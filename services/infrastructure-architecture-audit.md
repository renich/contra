# Service: Infrastructure & Cloud Architecture Audit

**Pricing Model**: Fixed Price ($2,500 – $5,000 depending on environment size)  
**Turnaround**: 5 to 7 business days  
**Delivery Vehicle**: Technical Brief, Architecture Diagram (D2/Mermaid), Remediation Roadmap  

---

## What This Solves
Growing engineering organizations frequently accumulate architectural debt: runaway cloud bills, fragile deployment scripts, single points of failure, and security vulnerabilities lingering unnoticed.

This audit provides a ruthless, zero-assumption forensic evaluation of your production infrastructure, uncovering reliability bottlenecks, cost waste, and security holes before they trigger downtime or compliance failures.

---

## Scope of Inspection

1. **Topology & High Availability**:
   - VPC/network design, ingress/egress bottlenecks, multi-region/AZ resilience.
   - Redundancy, failover mechanics, and disaster recovery readiness.
2. **Container & Compute Orchestration**:
   - Container daemon security, rootless container posture, resource requests/limits.
   - Systemd integration, process supervision, and restart policies.
3. **Security Posture & Compliance**:
   - Host-level hardening, SELinux/AppArmor enforcement, secret lifecycle management.
   - Firewall rules, PKI/TLS certificate hygiene, and least-privilege IAM policies.
4. **CI/CD & Release Pipeline Health**:
   - Build velocity, test isolation, caching efficiency, and supply chain security.
5. **Cost Optimization**:
   - Idle resource identification, over-provisioned instances, unattached storage volumes, and egress cost drains.

---

## Deliverables

1. **Executive Architecture Brief**: High-level summary of risks, technical debt score, and cost reduction potential.
2. **Technical Findings & Remediation Roadmap**: Prioritized, step-by-step action plan categorized by urgency (P0: Critical Security/Outage Risks, P1: Efficiency/Reliability, P2: Polish/Optimization).
3. **Architecture Diagrams**: Clean declarative diagrams documenting current vs. recommended target topology.
4. **45-Minute Review & Strategy Session**: Direct walkthrough with your engineering leadership.
